🚗 Car Management System

Complete fleet & service management — built for Odoo 19

Car Management System is a fully featured Odoo module that lets you track your entire vehicle fleet — from brands and models to ownership records, service history, and financial reporting — all in one place. It integrates natively with Odoo Accounting so service invoices are created automatically.

✨ Key Features

🏷️ Brands & Models

Maintain a structured catalogue of car brands and models. Each model is linked to its brand for easy filtering.

🚙 Vehicle Registry

Store VIN, license plate, year, colour, mileage and owner for every vehicle in your fleet.

🔧 Service Records

Log maintenance and repair jobs with dates, technicians, parts, labour costs and status tracking.

💳 Invoice Integration

Generate Odoo invoices directly from service records with one click — no double entry required.

📊 Reporting

Built-in PDF reports: Vehicle Card, Service History, and Fleet Summary — all printable from the interface.

📬 Chatter & Activity

Every vehicle and service record has a full Odoo chatter for notes, attachments, and scheduled activities.

📂 Module Structure

Menu What you can do
Vehicles → All Vehicles Create, search, and edit the complete vehicle registry
Services → Service Records Log and track every maintenance or repair job
Reporting → Service Analysis Pivot / graph analysis of service costs by vehicle or period
Reporting → Fleet Overview Dashboard-style overview of the whole fleet
Configuration → Brands / Models Manage the brand and model catalogue
